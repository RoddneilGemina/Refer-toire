Built for Choral Directors & Choristers

Zero complicated logins. Instant code pairing, high-fidelity score rendering, and stage glare reduction.

🔑

Access Code Instant Sync

Singers join by entering their choir's 8-character access code. The app instantly verifies against the database and synchronizes all repertoire scores offline.

🎼

Mozilla PDF.js Score Viewer

High-DPI canvas rendering with native devicePixelRatio scaling. Features single-page turns, continuous scroll, precision zoom (60%–250%), and foot pedal support.

🎭

Concert Hall Stage Mode

Inverts sheet music to high-contrast white & gold notation on an ultra-dark background to eliminate face glare and preserve night vision on darkened concert stages.

⚡

100% Offline Storage

Scores are cached in persistent local storage. Rehearsals in ancient cathedrals or underground rehearsal halls continue without disruption.

📚

Choral Voicing & Season Filters

Filter repertoire by SATB, SSAA, TTBB, Liturgical Season (Advent, Lent, Easter, Ordinary Time), composer surname, and conductor rehearsal notes.

📤

File-First PDF Uploads

Directors can upload scores with automatic title parsing and instant broadcast to all ensemble choristers via Supabase Cloud Storage.
